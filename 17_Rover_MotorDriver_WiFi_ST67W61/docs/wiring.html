GrassKit Rover — NUCLEO-L073RZ + X-NUCLEO-67W61M1 → X-STM32MP-RBT01 → 4 × DC wheel motors

14 jumper wires carry logic between the stacked Nucleo / ST67W61 Wi-Fi 6 pair and the RBT01's 40-pin CN2 header; two heavy wires bring bench power into CN3; four motors land on CN4 (front) and CN5 (rear). Each label gives the physical spot to count to: on the Nucleo hold the USB at the top, on the RBT01 hold CN2 along the top with the silver capacitor on the left. Signals on the shield's Arduino pins can be taken from the ST67W61 top sockets (dashed) or the Morpho pin underneath.

Signal chain: STM32L073RZ TIM3 CH1-CH4 (PC6 / PB5 / PB0 / PB1) + DIR (PC0-PC3) + EN/FAULT (PC4 / PC5 / PC9 / PC8) → RBT01 CN2 → STSPIN948 U2 (M1, M2 → CN4) and U3 (M3, M4 → CN5). Bench supply → CN3 VM / GND. Nucleo on its own ST-LINK USB (WebApp via USART2, 115200 baud).
