Sheet A · Motors and power, soldered

Solder each motor's two leads to the pin tails on the underside of the board. Numbers 1–8 are the eight motor joints, 9–10 the motor power.

Signal chain: STM32L073RZ TIM3 CH1–CH4 (PC6 / PB5 / PB0 / PB1) for speed, PC0–PC3 for direction, PC4 / PC5 / PC9 / PC8 for enable → RBT01 CN2 → STSPIN948 U2 (M1, M2 → CN4) and U3 (M3, M4 → CN5). Motor power → CN3. Forward with the default settings drives the 2 pin high, so current enters each motor's black lead.
